Delete a profile or home

You can delete your Together profile or ask the household organizer to remove another profile. This page is public so you can find these steps even if you have uninstalled the Android app.

Before you continue

Profile deletion removes that sign-in and profile. The organizer can choose whether assigned tasks are reassigned to the organizer or deleted. Shared task records may remain, with the deleted profile’s creator/completion name changed to “Former member.”

Deleting a home permanently removes all profiles, tasks, and recurring schedules in that home.

Delete your own profile

  1. Open Together and sign in to your home and profile.
  2. Open the profile menu at the top of the dashboard.
  3. Choose Delete my profile, enter your profile PIN, and confirm.

Delete another family profile

An organizer can choose Delete a family profile in the profile menu, verify with their PIN, and choose whether the profile’s assigned tasks should be reassigned or deleted.

Delete the entire home

The organizer can choose Delete this home, enter their profile PIN, and type the home name to confirm permanent deletion.

If you can’t sign in

Use the home recovery options on the sign-in screen, or ask the organizer/backup organizer to help restore access. For your safety, deletion requires the relevant home or profile credentials.
